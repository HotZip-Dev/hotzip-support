OnlyRares

Alerts you when a rare mob is nearby, with a one-click target button, and pins every rare you've seen on the world map and minimap. Learns what each rare drops from your own loot.

Latest: 1.0.0
Get it on Wago Get it on CurseForge

OnlyRares alerts you when a rare mob is nearby, gives you a one-click button to target it, and pins every rare you've seen on your world map and minimap.

Features

  • Rare alerts. When a rare shows up on a nameplate, as your target or mouseover, or as a map marker, you get a sound, a raid-warning line, a chat line and a small alert window. Each spawn alerts you once.
  • One-click target. The alert window's Target button targets the rare. Drops shows what it has dropped for you. Broadcast posts its location to party chat with a clickable map pin (only while you're in a party). Drag the window to move it, right-click to dismiss it. It closes itself after 90 seconds.
  • Combat safe. In combat the sound and raid-warning line still play right away. The window appears as soon as combat ends.
  • Rare tracker. Every rare you see is saved with where and when you saw it. Pins show "last seen", how often it was seen at that spot, "last killed" and the fastest respawn you've observed. Gold stars are rare elites, silver stars are rares.
  • World map pins. Left-click a pin to set a waypoint, right-click to see the rare's drops, Shift+right-click to forget that spot.
  • Minimap pins. Nearby rares show on your minimap with the same tooltip plus the distance. The 2 nearest rares out of range sit on the minimap's edge with their distance in yards. Rotating and square minimaps work too.
  • Drops learned from your loot. Every time you loot a rare, its items are recorded with how often they dropped (for example "3/5 60%"). Nothing is pre-loaded, so it stays accurate as Forever adds new rare drops. Shift-click an item to link it in chat, Ctrl-click to preview it.
  • Party sync. Party members who also run OnlyRares share sightings and kills automatically. Everyone gets the map pin and the kill time, plus an alert ("spotted by ...") when they're in the same zone.
  • Ignore list. Stop alerts and pins for a rare you don't care about.
  • Minimap button. Left-click shows or hides all pins, right-click shows or hides only the minimap pins. Drag it around the minimap's edge.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/or or /onlyraresHow many rares you've recorded, plus the command list
/or listEvery recorded rare, most recent first
/or drops <name>Open a rare's recorded drops (no name uses your target)
/or testShow a test alert so you can check the sound and move the window
/or soundTurn the alert sound on or off
/or syncTurn party sync on or off (on by default)
/or show, /or hide, /or toggleShow or hide all world map and minimap pins
/or minimapShow or hide the minimap pins only
/or buttonShow or hide the minimap button
/or ignore <name>Stop alerts and pins for a rare (no name uses your target; with neither, lists ignored rares)
/or unignore <name>Undo an ignore
/or forget <name>Delete a recorded rare
/or clear confirmDelete every recorded rare

Good to know

  • Your rares, drops and settings are saved per account, so all your characters share them.
  • Only rares you (or your party) have seen get pins. Nothing is pre-loaded.
  • Turn on enemy nameplates (V) so rares are spotted from further away.
