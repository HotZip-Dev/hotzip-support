Plans your Enchanting leveling from 1 to 300: the current step, the mats you still need, where to get them and what they cost on the Auction House.

Features

  • A ready-made 1-300 route. Built for Forever, with alternatives for each skill range, including options that skip a formula or a rod you don't have yet. 1-225 is tested on Forever; 225-300 follows the Classic route and is marked untested in the plan.
  • Tracker. A small window with your current step, about how many crafts are left, the mats for it (have / need) and your next milestone: training, or a formula to buy. Click the recipe to open it in your Professions window.
  • Your plan, your way. Switch any range to one of its alternatives, change craft counts, or skip steps. Hover a step for its mats and tips.
  • Shopping list. Every mat you still need, minus what's in your bags and bank, with the best source and the last AH price you saw. Show it up to your next training, or for the whole plan.
  • Source cards. Click any mat to see where it comes from (vendor, disenchanting by level, farming, other professions), how many you have and its last AH price.
  • AH prices. Whenever you browse or search the Auction House, OnlyEnchants notes the lowest price of any mat on the route. Or press Check prices in the shopping list while the AH is open to look them all up.
  • Cheapest option. Every step shows its AH cost, green when your pick is the cheapest and orange when another option for that range is cheaper.
  • Rod warnings. If a step needs a Runed Rod you don't have, or yours is in the bank, the tracker says so and offers an option that works with what you have. A rod you're missing adds its mats to the shopping list. The Runed Arcanite Rod is optional, since no leveling recipe needs it.
  • Skill cap and formula reminders. The tracker tells you when you've hit your cap and need to train. A formula you skipped buying stays as a reminder until you know the recipe.
  • Disenchanting help. Green, blue and purple gear shows what it disenchants into right on its tooltip, plus what you actually got from items like it. The Disenchanting tab has the full table, with the mats your plan needs highlighted.
  • Minimap button. Left-click opens the plan, right-click shows or hides the tracker, drag it to move it around the minimap. Its tooltip shows your skill and current step.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/oeOpen the plan (/onlyenchants and /oe plan also work)
/oe listOpen the shopping list
/oe deOpen the disenchanting table
/oe trackerShow or hide the tracker
/oe buttonShow or hide the minimap button
/oe resetUndo your route changes and go back to the default route

Good to know

  • Nothing leaves your game. OnlyEnchants sends no addon messages and posts nothing to chat. AH prices come only from searches you make, or from Check prices, which searches the AH one mat at a time while it's open.
  • Settings and window positions are shared across your account. Route changes and known formulas are saved per character, and AH prices per realm.
  • The tracker and minimap button only show on characters with Enchanting. Once the plan is complete, the tracker hides itself; /oe still opens the plan.
  • Some farming sources come from Classic and are marked "not confirmed on Forever" until they're checked.
