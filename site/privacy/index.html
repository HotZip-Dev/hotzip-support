Privacy

Short version: our addons keep your data on your computer, and this site doesn't track you.

The addons

  • OnlyOres and OnlyHerbs save the spots you gather in your own SavedVariables. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • OnlyEnchants saves your plan, route changes and the Auction House prices you've seen in your own SavedVariables. It sends no messages and posts nothing to chat. Prices only come from Auction House searches you make, or from the Check prices button while the Auction House is open.
  • OnlyNotes saves your notes on your own computer. It sends no messages, and other players can never see your notes.
  • OnlyRares saves the rares you see, their drops and your settings in your own SavedVariables. With party sync on (the default) and while you're in a party, each rare you spot or kill is shared with your party through the hidden addon channel: its name, level, type, NPC ID, spawn ID and map position. Nothing goes outside your party, and /or sync turns it off. The Broadcast button posts a normal party chat message, only when you click it.
  • WowTogether shares your player card with other WowTogether players on your realm and faction, through a hidden in-game chat channel. Only your zone is shared, never your exact position, and your name stays hidden until you both match. You can stop showing your card at any time.

This site

  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers.
  • It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

The support form

  • What you type becomes a public report on GitHub. Don't include personal info.
  • Your IP address is used briefly to stop spam (a few reports per hour) and isn't stored with your report.
