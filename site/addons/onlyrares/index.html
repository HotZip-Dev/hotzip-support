OnlyRares

Alerts you when a rare mob is nearby, with a one-click target button, and pins every rare you've seen on the world map and minimap. Keeps track of what each rare drops for you.

Latest: 1.0.0
Get it on Wago Get it on CurseForge

Rares are easy to miss and hard to find again. OnlyRares tells you the moment one is nearby, and remembers where it spawned and what it dropped.

Features

  • Rare alerts. When a rare is nearby, you get a sound, a raid-warning line, a chat line and a small alert window. Each spawn alerts you once.
  • One-click target. The alert window's Target button targets the rare, Drops shows what it has dropped for you, and Broadcast posts its location to party chat with a clickable map pin.
  • World map pins. Every rare you see gets a pin: gold stars for rare elites, silver for rares. Hover for when it was last seen and killed, and the fastest respawn you've seen. Click for a waypoint, right-click for its drops.
  • Minimap pins. Nearby rares show on your minimap with their distance, and the nearest ones farther out wait on its edge.
  • Drops from your loot. Every time you loot a rare, its items are recorded with how often they dropped (for example "3/5 60%").
  • Party sync. Party members who also use OnlyRares see your sightings and kills, and you see theirs. When one of them spots a rare in your zone, you get an alert too.
  • Ignore list. Stop alerts and pins for a rare you don't care about.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/or or /onlyraresHow many rares you've recorded, plus the command list
/or listEvery recorded rare, most recent first
/or drops <name>Open a rare's recorded drops (no name uses your target)
/or testPreview the alert, to check the sound and move the window
/or soundTurn the alert sound on or off
/or syncTurn party sync on or off (on by default)
/or show, /or hide, /or toggleShow or hide all world map and minimap pins
/or minimapShow or hide the minimap pins only
/or buttonShow or hide the minimap button
/or ignore <name>Stop alerts and pins for a rare (no name uses your target, or lists ignored rares)
/or unignore <name>Undo an ignore
/or forget <name>Delete a recorded rare
/or clear confirmDelete every recorded rare

Good to know

  • Turn on enemy nameplates (V) so you spot rares from farther away.
  • All your characters share your rares, drops and settings.
  • Your map starts empty and fills in as you and your party find rares.
