Privacy

Short version: our addons keep your data on your computer, and this site doesn't track you.

The addons

  • OnlyOres and OnlyHerbs save the spots you gather in your own SavedVariables. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • WowTogether shares your player card with other WowTogether players on your realm and faction, through a hidden in-game chat channel. Only your zone is shared, never your exact position, and your name stays hidden until you both match. You can stop showing your card at any time.

This site

  • No cookies, no analytics, no trackers.
  • It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

The support form

  • What you type becomes a public report on GitHub. Don't include personal info.
  • Your IP address is used briefly to stop spam (a few reports per hour) and isn't stored with your report.
