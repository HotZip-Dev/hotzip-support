OnlyEnchants

Plans your Enchanting leveling from 1 to 300: the current step, the mats you still need, where to get them and what they cost on the Auction House.

Latest: 1.0.2
Get it on Wago Get it on CurseForge

OnlyEnchants walks you from 1 to 300 one step at a time: what to craft next, what to buy, and what it costs, so you don't waste mats or gold.

Features

  • A ready-made 1-300 route. Built for Forever, with alternatives for each skill range, including options that skip a formula or a rod you don't have yet.
  • Tracker. A small window with your current step, about how many crafts are left, the mats for it and your next milestone: training, or a formula to buy. Click the recipe to open it in your Professions window.
  • Your plan, your way. Switch any range to one of its alternatives, change craft counts, or skip steps. Hover a step for its mats and tips.
  • Shopping list. Every mat you still need, minus what's in your bags and bank, with the best place to get it and its Auction House price. Show it up to your next training, or for the whole plan.
  • Auction House prices. Every step shows what it costs, so you can see which option is cheapest. Prices fill in as you search the AH, or press Check prices on the Shopping list tab to look them all up.
  • Source cards. Click any mat to see where it comes from (vendor, disenchanting, farming, other professions), how many you have and what it costs.
  • Rod warnings. If a step needs a Runed Rod you don't have, or yours is in the bank, the tracker says so and offers an option that works with what you have. A rod you're missing adds its mats to your shopping list.
  • Disenchanting help. Green, blue and purple gear shows what it disenchants into right on its tooltip, plus what you actually got from items like it. The Disenchanting tab has the full table, with the mats your plan needs highlighted.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/oeOpen the plan (/onlyenchants and /oe plan also work)
/oe listOpen the shopping list
/oe deOpen the disenchanting table
/oe trackerShow or hide the tracker
/oe buttonShow or hide the minimap button
/oe resetUndo your route changes and go back to the default route

Good to know

  • The tracker and minimap button only show on characters with Enchanting.
  • Left-click the minimap button to open the plan. Right-click it to show or hide the tracker.
