WowTogether

Find a leveling buddy nearby. Go through anonymous player cards, match on shared quests and playstyle, and whisper when you both say yes. Names stay hidden until you match.

Latest: 0.2.1
Get it on Wago

WowTogether helps you find a leveling buddy nearby who plays like you do: same quests, same pace, same hours, same sense of humor. Leveling is better with a buddy.

How it works

  1. Set up your card in a few minutes: your mood, playstyle, play times, a short bio, and a 13-question quiz that finds your player type (Completionist, Wanderer, Guildie or Duelist).
  2. Go through cards of other WowTogether players, one at a time. No names, just level, class, zone, a short bio, the quests you share and what you have in common. Pick Pass or Match. Players you pass come back later.
  3. When you both pick Match, it's a match! You see each other's names, and a popup offers a ready-made whisper that mentions a quest you share. Whisper or invite them from the Matches tab.

Features

  • Best matches first. Players on your quests and with your playstyle show up first.
  • Shared quests on every card. You see which quests you both have, so you know you have something to do together.
  • Daily mood. "Feeling chatty", "Need help with a quest" and more show at the top of your card for 12 hours. Right-click the minimap heart to change it.
  • Player quiz. Your player type shows on your card. Your answers never do.
  • Hard passes. Mark what you'd rather avoid, like politics, drama or rushing everything. They never show on your card.
  • Level range. See players within 2, 3, 5 or 10 levels of you, or any level.
  • Matches and History. Whisper, invite or unmatch from Matches. Rematch past matches from History.
  • Minimap heart. Click to open, right-click to set your mood. Hover it to see who's online and your matches.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/wtOpen or close the window (/wowtogether also works)
/wt setupSet up your card again
/wt quizTake or retake the player quiz
/wt minimapHide or show the minimap button
/wt demoAdd 5 practice players to try matching on your own (run it again to remove them)
/wt helpList every command

Good to know

  • You only see players who also run WowTogether, on your realm and faction, while they're online.
  • Players on your WoW ignore list never show up.
  • Turn off "Show my card to others" or "Share my quests" in Profile anytime.
  • Each of your characters has its own card and matches.
  • Uses one of your chat channel slots.
