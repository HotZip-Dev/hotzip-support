G Suite

Everything your guild runs on, in one window: raids and signups, gear, loot, events, crafting, the guild bank and officer tools.

Latest: 1.0.1
Get it on Wago

Running a guild means chasing signups, gear checks and bank requests across spreadsheets and chat. G Suite puts all of it in one window your whole guild shares.

Features

  • Raids and signups. Post a raid and guildmates sign up with their role and spec, or as late, tentative or bench. Everyone signed up gets a reminder before it starts.
  • Build the comp. Drag players into groups, publish it, and everyone sees whether they're in. Form group invites the players you placed.
  • Discord signups come along. Add your Discord User ID to your profile. When a raid leader pastes a Discord event's export, everyone who signed up there is signed up here too.
  • Gear and best in slot. See what your guildmates wear, and keep best in slot lists for every class and spec. Each player sees what they still need.
  • Loot. Rolls, reserves, loot history, attendance and points, all in one place.
  • The guild bank for everyone. Members see what's in the guild bank, even if their rank can't open its tabs. Gold in and out shows up in the treasury on its own, sorted into funds your guild picks.
  • Guild life. Post events, from guild meetings to fishing contests. Run polls, thank a guildmate with kudos, find a group for a dungeon, and see who can craft what.
  • Your guild, your setup. Officers choose which parts the guild uses and who can do what. Everything else stays out of the way.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/gsOpen or close G Suite
/gs bankOpen the guild bank
/gs leadOpen the raid lead panel
/gs checkOpen the raid check
/gs buffsOpen the raid buffs window
/gs lootOpen the loot window
/gs pull 10Start a pull timer (/gs pull 0 cancels)
/gs lfgSwitch Available in the group finder on or off
/gs kudos NameThank a guildmate
/gs char NameShow a guildmate's gear, talents and professions
/gs icons, /gs hubSwitch between the icon rail and the home hub layout
/gs minimapShow or hide the minimap button

Good to know

  • Your guildmates need G Suite too, to see what you post and to sign up.
  • The first officer to open G Suite sets it up for the guild. Until then, everything works with standard settings.
