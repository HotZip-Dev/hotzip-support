OnlySplits

OnlySplits times every level you gain against your best run, shows where each minute went, and helps you record, refine and follow a quest plan.

What are splits?

Speedrunners break a run into checkpoints called splits and time each one. Comparing every split with your personal best (PB) shows where you gained or lost time, not just your final total.

In leveling, every level is a split. You see that level 23 took 12 minutes longer than last time, and why: a long walk back to town, a death, a slow quest. Fix those spots and your next character levels faster. Not racing? It still shows where your playtime goes.

Features

  • Level splits. Every ding is a split in played time, next to your best time for that level on your other characters.
  • Where the time went. Each level splits into combat, travel, town, dead, idle and flight time, with its biggest time sink highlighted. You also see quest and kill XP, rested bonus, deaths and hearthstones.
  • Quests and town stops. See how long each quest took and its XP per minute, slowest first. Vendor and trainer visits show the gold you spent and the spells you learned.
  • Split bar. Your level, time against your best, XP per hour, time to level and your plan's next steps. Hover it for this level's breakdown.
  • Level report. Click a level for a Report and a Timeline of every event. Copy turns your splits, reports and plans into text you can paste into Discord or your notes.
  • Record a plan. Record new builds a plan while you play: your quests, vendor and trainer stops, and the progress you make on the way. Every step shows how long it took you and how that compares with your best.
  • Follow a plan. Load plan shows your next steps and flags any you missed. It warns before training you can't afford and reminds you when training is due.
  • Plan Builder. Drag steps, add quests by name, make a plan from part of a run, and set a default plan for your race and class. Each quest shows its level, quest giver and turn-in NPC.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/osOpen or close the splits window (/onlysplits also works)
/os stop, /os startStop or start logging this character
/os barShow or hide the split bar
/os buttonShow or hide the minimap button
/os pb, /os pb allCompare with your same-class characters, or with all of them

Good to know

  • Times are played time, so logged-out time never counts. You'll see the usual /played lines in chat.
  • All your characters count toward your PBs and share your plans.
  • A new character asks once: record a new plan, load one, or don't log this character.
  • Left-click the minimap button for the splits window, right-click it to show or hide the bar.
