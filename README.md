# HotZip Addons support

Report a bug or share an idea at [hotzip.dev/support](https://hotzip.dev/support/). No account needed.

HotZip Addons are made for World of Warcraft: Forever.

## Addons

| Addon | What it does |
|---|---|
| OnlyOres | Remembers where you mined ore and shows it on your map |
| OnlyHerbs | Remembers where you picked herbs and shows it on your map |
| OnlyEnchants | Plans your Enchanting leveling from 1 to 300 |
| OnlyNotes | Your own notes on players, NPCs, items and map spots |
| OnlyRares | Rare mob alerts and kill tracking |
| OnlySplits | Speed leveling splits, where your time went, and quest plans |
| WowTogether | Find leveling buddies |

## A good bug report has

- the addon and its version (shown in the in-game AddOns list)
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened
- any error text. Turn errors on with `/console scriptErrors 1`, or use BugSack.
