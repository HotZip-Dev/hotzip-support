OnlyOres

Mine a node and OnlyOres remembers it: pins on your world map and minimap, a last-mined timer, and distance to nearby veins.

Latest: 1.0.3
Get it on Wago Get it on CurseForge

OnlyOres remembers every ore node you mine and puts it on your map, so you can build your own mining routes and see how long ago each spot was last mined.

Features

  • Automatic recording. Mine a node and its spot is saved. No setup, no imports.
  • World map pins. Each saved spot shows the icon of the ore you last mined there. Hover to see the ore and how long ago you last mined it.
  • Minimap rings. Nearby spots show as a ring in the ore's color. When the vein is up, the Find Minerals dot appears inside the ring, so you can tell at a glance whether it's there.
  • Distance to the next vein. The nearest spots out of minimap range stick to the minimap's edge with their distance in yards, like party member arrows.
  • Shared spawn points. When the same spot rolls different ores (Tin/Silver, Iron/Gold), they stay on one pin, and its tooltip lists every ore you've seen there.
  • Waypoints. Left-click a world map pin to set a waypoint to it. Shift + right-click a pin to forget that one spot.
  • Ignore list. Tell OnlyOres to skip a node type you don't care about, and its pins are removed.
  • Minimap button. Click to show or hide pins. Drag it anywhere around the minimap's edge.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/oo or /onlyoresHow many spots you've saved, plus the command list
/oo show, /oo hide, /oo toggleShow or hide all pins
/oo minimapShow or hide minimap pins only
/oo buttonShow or hide the minimap button
/oo ignore <node name>Stop recording a node type and remove its pins (full name, like Copper Vein)
/oo ignoreList the node types you're ignoring
/oo unignore <node name>Start recording it again
/oo clearDelete every saved spot (asks you to type /oo clear confirm)

Good to know

  • Your map starts empty and fills in as you mine.
  • All your characters share one map.
  • Nodes inside dungeons aren't recorded.
