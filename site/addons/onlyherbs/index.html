OnlyHerbs

Pick an herb and OnlyHerbs remembers it: pins on your world map and minimap, a last-picked timer, and distance to nearby herbs.

Latest: v1.0.2
Get it on Wago Get it on CurseForge

OnlyHerbs remembers every herb you pick and puts it on your map, so you can build your own herbalism routes and see how long ago each spot was last picked clean.

Features

  • Automatic recording. Pick an herb and its spot is saved. No setup, no imports.
  • World map pins. Each saved spot shows the herb's icon. Hover to see the herb, how long ago you last picked it, and how many times you've harvested it.
  • Minimap rings. Nearby spots show as a ring colored by herb. When the herb is up, the Find Herbs dot appears inside the ring, so you can tell at a glance whether it's there.
  • Distance to the next herb. The 3 nearest spots out of minimap range stick to the minimap's edge with their distance in yards, like party member arrows. Hover any minimap pin for the herb, its distance and when you last picked it.
  • Shared spawn points. When the same spot grows different herbs, they stay on one pin, and the tooltip lists every herb you've seen there.
  • Waypoints. Left-click a world map pin to set a waypoint to it. Shift + right-click a pin to forget that one spot.
  • Ignore list. Tell OnlyHerbs to skip an herb you don't care about, and its pins are removed.
  • Minimap button. Left-click to show or hide all pins, right-click for minimap pins only. Drag it anywhere around the minimap. Hover it to see how many spots you've saved.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/oh or /onlyherbsHow many spots you've saved, plus the command list
/oh show, /oh hide, /oh toggleShow or hide all pins
/oh minimapShow or hide minimap pins only
/oh buttonShow or hide the minimap button
/oh ignore <herb name>Stop recording an herb and remove its pins (full name, like Khadgar's Whisker)
/oh ignoreList the herbs you're ignoring
/oh unignore <herb name>Start recording it again
/oh clearDelete every saved spot (type /oh clear confirm to go through with it)

Good to know

  • Nothing leaves your game. Only herbs you pick are recorded. OnlyHerbs doesn't scan the world, send addon messages or share data with anyone.
  • Your data is saved per account, so all your characters build the same map.
  • Herbs picked inside dungeons and other instances aren't recorded, since the game gives no map position there.
  • Herbs OnlyHerbs doesn't know yet, like new Forever ones, are still recorded. They get the Herbalism icon and a white ring.
  • It's the sister addon of OnlyOres, which does the same for mining. The two run side by side.
