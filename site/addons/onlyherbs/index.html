OnlyHerbs

Pick an herb and OnlyHerbs remembers it: pins on your world map and minimap, a last-picked timer, and distance to nearby herbs.

Latest: 1.0.3
Get it on Wago Get it on CurseForge

OnlyHerbs remembers every herb you pick and puts it on your map, so you can build your own herbalism routes and see how long ago each spot was last picked.

Features

  • Automatic recording. Pick an herb and its spot is saved. No setup, no imports.
  • World map pins. Each saved spot shows the icon of the herb you last picked there. Hover to see the herb and how long ago you last picked it.
  • Minimap rings. Nearby spots show as a ring in the herb's color. When the herb is up, the Find Herbs dot appears inside the ring, so you can tell at a glance whether it's there.
  • Distance to the next herb. The nearest spots out of minimap range stick to the minimap's edge with their distance in yards, like party member arrows.
  • Shared spawn points. When the same spot grows different herbs, they stay on one pin, and its tooltip lists every herb you've seen there.
  • Waypoints. Left-click a world map pin to set a waypoint to it. Shift + right-click a pin to forget that one spot.
  • Ignore list. Tell OnlyHerbs to skip an herb you don't care about, and its pins are removed.
  • Minimap button. Click to show or hide pins. Drag it anywhere around the minimap's edge.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/oh or /onlyherbsHow many spots you've saved, plus the command list
/oh show, /oh hide, /oh toggleShow or hide all pins
/oh minimapShow or hide minimap pins only
/oh buttonShow or hide the minimap button
/oh ignore <herb name>Stop recording an herb and remove its pins (full name, like Khadgar's Whisker)
/oh ignoreList the herbs you're ignoring
/oh unignore <herb name>Start recording it again
/oh clearDelete every saved spot (asks you to type /oh clear confirm)

Good to know

  • Your map starts empty and fills in as you pick.
  • All your characters share one map.
  • Herbs inside dungeons aren't recorded.
