OnlyNotes

Your own wiki in WoW: write notes on players, NPCs, items, map spots and free pages, then see them in tooltips, in chat and on your map.

Latest: 1.0.2
Get it on Wago Get it on CurseForge

Remember who was great to group with, which NPC sold that recipe, and where you saw that rare spawn. OnlyNotes keeps it all and shows it right where you need it.

Features

  • A page for anything. Players, NPCs, items and map spots each get one page, and you can make free pages like "MC strats" or "Gold routes". Player and NPC pages show the class, level, guild or zone you last saw.
  • Quick add. Right-click a player or NPC and choose Add note. Your first note becomes the page's summary, and later ones are added as dated journal lines.
  • Summary where you need it. A page's summary shows in that player's, NPC's or item's tooltip, and next to a player's name in chat.
  • Map notes. Alt+click a zone on the world map, or use /note here, to pin a note to a spot on the world map and minimap. Click a pin to open its page, or right-click it to add a line or set a waypoint.
  • Journal lines. Type in the Add a line box under any page to add a dated line without opening the editor.
  • Links. Shift-click items, quests or spells into a page. Hover them later for the tooltip, or click an item to open its page.
  • Find anything. Search titles and text, or filter by Players, NPCs, Items, Places or Pages.
  • Your call what shows. Turn Show in tooltips, Show in chat and Show on map on or off in the window.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
/noteOpen or close the window (also /onlynotes)
/note BobOpen Bob's page (Bob-Realm for another realm)
/note [item]Open an item's page (shift-click the item into the command)
/note <title>Open a free page by its title
/note new <title>Make a free page
/note hereAdd a note at your position
/note buttonShow or hide the minimap button
/note helpList all commands

Good to know

  • Click the minimap button to open the window, or right-click it for a new page or a note where you stand.
  • All your characters share the same pages.
  • /note here and minimap pins don't work inside instances.
